## 🚀 Project Overview
**Logística** is an end-to-end fleet management and analytics project designed to measure operational efficiency, track revenue and Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), monitor fuel consumption, and evaluate truck and customer performance to drive data-backed logistics decisions.

---

## 🗄️ Data Architecture & ETL
The project relies on a robust **Star Schema** linking fact tables and dimension tables:
* **Fact Tables:** Freight & shipments data (`fFreight`) and distance/costs tracking data (`fKMTraveled`).
* **Dimension Tables:** Customers (`Customers`), vehicles and trucks (`Vehicles`), and drivers (`Drivers`).

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Net Revenue:** Tracking total net earnings and performance trends.
* **Total Cost & Cost per KM:** Monitoring comprehensive operational expenses and fleet maintenance costs.
* **Profit Margin:** Measuring overall profitability margins across logistics operations.
* **Fleet Utilization:** Monitoring asset efficiency and payload tracking (Kg & Cubic capacity).
